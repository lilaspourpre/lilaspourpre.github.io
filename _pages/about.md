I am a postdoctoral researcher at [Mohamed bin Zayed University of Artificial Intelligence (MBZUAI)](https://mbzuai.ac.ae/) in Abu Dhabi, hosted by [Alham Fikri Aji](https://scholar.google.ca/citations?user=0Cyfqv4AAAAJ). I joined MBZUAI in September 2026.

My research interests include taxonomies and lexical semantics, question answering, argument mining, retrieval-augmented generation, and NLP for low-resource languages.

Previously, I was a postdoctoral researcher in the Language Technology Group at Universität Hamburg (August 2022–September 2025), hosted by Chris Biemann, working on comparative question answering in the ACQuA-2.0 project. From September 2025 to September 2026, I continued as an independent researcher, collaborating with MBZUAI and supervising students at Hamburg and HSE.

I received my PhD in Computational and Data Science and Engineering from Skoltech in 2022, supervised by [Alexander Panchenko](https://faculty.skoltech.ru/people/alexanderpanchenko). My thesis, *Learning Linguistic Tree Structures with Text and Graph Methods*, focused on taxonomy enrichment.
